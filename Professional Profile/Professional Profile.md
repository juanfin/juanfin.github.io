# Professional Profile

Economist with an emphasis in finance and macroeconomics. I have worked in transfer pricing and financial advisory at Deloitte and Andersen, and in macroeconomic research at Banco de la República, Colombia's central bank. My main interest is financial modelling: valuing companies through comparable analysis, EBITDA multiples and discounted cash flow, and reading those results against the macroeconomic picture of inflation, interest rates and employment. This portfolio collects the models and charts I build to practise both sides.
