# Financial Portfolio

Financial and economic models built from public data.

## Finance

### 1. Comparable Company Analysis

Amazon (tested party) against seven US technology companies: Microsoft, Nvidia, Alphabet, Meta Platforms, Apple, Broadcom and ServiceNow. Fiscal year 2025.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| [Tech Comps - Financial Statements FY2025.xlsx](<Finance/1. Comparable Company Analysis/Tech Comps - Financial Statements FY2025.xlsx>) | Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement for each company in one standard layout, with a link to each Form 10-K |
| [Tech Comps - Profitability Ratios FY2025.xlsx](<Finance/1. Comparable Company Analysis/Tech Comps - Profitability Ratios FY2025.xlsx>) | Gross margin, operating margin, ROA and ROE, with the interquartile range of the comparables and Amazon's position against it |

Source: U.S. SEC EDGAR XBRL company facts, from each company's Form 10-K. The caveats are listed in the README sheet of the statements file and the Notes sheet of the ratios file.

### 2. EBITDA Multiples

Enterprise value / EBITDA for the same seven comparables, with share prices at 31 December 2025, applied to Amazon's EBITDA to get an implied share price.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| [Tech Comps - EBITDA Multiples FY2025.xlsx](<Finance/2. EBITDA Multiples/Tech Comps - EBITDA Multiples FY2025.xlsx>) | Market capitalization, net debt, enterprise value, EV / EBITDA and EV / Revenue, and the implied valuation of Amazon |

Sources: U.S. SEC EDGAR for the financials; Yahoo Finance closing prices. This is an illustration of the method, not investment advice.

### 3. DCF

Discounted cash flow valuation of Amazon at 31 December 2025: five projected years in three scenarios, WACC from the CAPM, Gordon growth terminal value and a sensitivity table.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| [Amazon - DCF Model FY2025.xlsx](<Finance/3. DCF/Amazon - DCF Model FY2025.xlsx>) | Assumptions with a scenario switch, projected free cash flows, WACC, valuation, sensitivity and scenario results |

The projection assumptions are the author's own. This is an illustration of the method, not investment advice.
